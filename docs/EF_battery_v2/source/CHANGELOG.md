# EF battery v2.0: what changed and why

This log maps every finding in the two audits (protocol audit and audit of the actual forms, both dated 20 October 2026) to the change made in v2.0. "Open" means the item still needs pilot data or a decision from the study team; it cannot be fixed by editing the forms alone.

## Deployment blockers (forms audit §1)

| #   | Finding                                                                                 | v2.0 change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Status                              |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1   | W1/W2 oral stimuli printed on participant pages                                         | The master form is split into separate documents. `participant_booklet_X` holds only W3, W6 and W7 plus practice items. `facilitator_record_X` holds the spoken stimuli, the expected answers and the response capture. An automated check confirms that no W1/W2 sequence, W5 sentence or fluency letter appears in any participant booklet.                                                                                                                                               | Fixed                               |
| 2   | TMT layouts uncalibrated (Part A path 1,537.5 mm vs 1,132.7 mm); "End" used as a target | Six layouts are now selected from a seeded pool of 240 to match path length, turning and distractor proximity. All six path lengths fall between 1,238 and 1,278 mm (within ±2%). Part A ends at **25** and Part B at **13**; "Begin" and "End" are small labels, not targets. Sample trails added for both parts.                                                                                                                                                                          | Fixed (pilot equating still needed) |
| 3   | Plus–minus carries 14 / 3 / 7 across forms                                              | Every list now has each final digit exactly 3 times, giving 9/30 tens-crossings in Lists 1 and 2 of every form. List 3 has 4/15 crossings on each operation. Tens and units are identical across forms, so list means are identical (54.5 / 54.5 / 56.5). Numbers run 13–96 (no single-digit answers). No adjacent equal or consecutive numbers, and never more than 2 crossings in a row. Each list is on its own page. Practice numbers are below 13, so they never appear as test items. | Fixed                               |
| 4   | Verbal tasks uncalibrated                                                               | See W4 and W5 below.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Partly fixed; cloze norming open    |
| 5   | No seeds, exporter, answer keys or tests                                                | `ef.py` records seeds, writes all documents, answer keys and `stimuli.json` (the frozen stimuli plus the code hash), and runs 90 automated checks; the build stops on any failure. `--from-json` rebuilds the documents exactly from the frozen stimuli.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Fixed                               |

## Task-level findings

| Task  | Finding                                                                                | v2.0 change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| W1    | Repeats allowed only at length 9 (e.g. Form C ended 1–1–1)                             | One rule at every length: no repeated digit (length 9 is a permutation of 1–9), no adjacent ±1 pair, the two trials at a length differ in first and last digit, and no sequence is reused anywhere in the battery. One practice trial per part. Discontinue rule clarified (both trials of a length failed). Forward span is reclassified as a covariate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| W1/W2 | WAIS licensing                                                                         | All sequences are original and randomly generated, not WAIS items. The report no longer borrows WAIS-IV reliability for these versions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| W2    | Digit/letter balance varied by form; length 2 trivial                                  | Composition, first-item category and position pattern are fixed by slot in all forms. Within-category reordering (inversions) is fixed. Length 2 is used only for 2 practice trials; scored lengths are 3–8 (0–12). Letters are chosen to limit acoustic confusion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| W3    | Arithmetic and speed confound                                                          | All three list times and errors are reported. The switch cost is primary, with ratio and residual versions as sensitivity analyses.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| W4    | F reused in Forms A and B                                                              | Letter sets are now FAS / BHR / PMT, with no letter shared. FAS and BHR are the D-KEFS standard and alternate sets; PMT is study-chosen and must be equated in the pilot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| W4    | Semantic categories not parallel                                                       | "Animals" is used at every occasion: the same item at every occasion, with the practice effect modelled. Semantic fluency is a covariate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| W4    | "Countries" conflicted with the proper-noun rule                                       | Switching pairs are now common nouns only: fruits/furniture, vegetables/clothing, jobs/vehicles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| W4    | No scoring manual                                                                      | `scoring_key` now has explicit rules (roots, plurals, compounds, sub- and superordinate terms, language), switching indices (correct switches, switch ratio, set-loss errors, intrusions), blind double scoring and a fixed English response language.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| W5    | Low-cloze sentences; gap not final ("She brushed her \_\_\_ before bed")               | All 90 sentences rewritten as high-constraint candidates with sentence-final gaps. Every expected word is unique in the battery, and no stem contains another same-form item's expected word. Sentences are balanced across the six form × part cells for length (8.40–8.47 words) and expected-word frequency (Zipf 4.54–4.55). There are 2 practice items per part and 8 reserve items. 19 cross-form overlaps remain (a sentence containing another form's expected word, e.g. "sun"); they are reported in the diagnostics and can be removed when failed items are replaced after norming. **Cloze norming is required before use** (`cloze_norming_form`, criterion ≥ 80% dominant completion). |
| W5    | A/B/0 undefined; RT measurement                                                        | The A/B/0 codes are now defined, and every Part B item on the record sheet shows coding anchors (an alternative sensible completion → A, a related word → B). These anchors are illustrative, and the full coding manual is refined with pilot responses. RT runs from the facilitator's last word to response onset, measured from audio where possible. Prompt rule at 15 s. The W5 index is formed within the task first and re-standardised, so the task is neither double-weighted nor under-weighted against Stroop.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| W6    | Colour balance approximate; ceiling at 100; colour access                              | Exact 34/33/33 counts per page, with 0% congruent CW items (as before). Added: no immediate word or ink repetition down columns, no more than 3 identical items across a row, a last item that differs from the first (for the restart rule), a practice row per page, a colour-naming check page, a restart-from-top rule and Golden's error procedure (the examiner says "No" and the participant corrects). Score = items completed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| W6    | Golden IG sign reversed in composite                                                   | Inhibition composite now uses **+z(IG)**. A worked example is in the report.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| W7    | B−A confounded by geometry                                                             | Part A and Part B layouts are matched to the same path-length target. The primary index is the residual of Part 2 time on Part 1 time; B−A and B/A are sensitivity analyses only. Discontinue rule: 300 s.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| W8    | Researcher-written items labelled as ADEXI; "last month" window overlaps between waves | Replaced by the **official 14-item ADEXI**, with its wording, 1–5 scale and scoring unchanged. Given at T0 (optional at T2), not at every occasion. A separate state check (sleep, fatigue, concentration, stress, caffeine, medication, effort) is given at every occasion and is labelled as not an ability measure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Protocol-level findings

| Finding                                                      | v2.0 change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Identify EF decline relative to norms"                      | Removed. Scores are nonclinical relative indicators; no clinical norms are applied and no clinical flags are raised.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| "EF is stable" inferred from no significant change           | Replaced by three separate questions: rank-order ICC, practice-adjusted mean change, and TOST equivalence against a per-metric SESOI set from the pilot. Inconclusive results are reported as inconclusive.                                                                                                   |
| Proportion with \|RCI\| < 1.96 used as a stability criterion | Removed. A practice-adjusted RCI is used only with independent retest data on these forms.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Form order ABC/BCA/CAB and "A at baseline" contradiction     | Occasion and form are now separated. Six orders are allocated in permuted blocks (`allocation.csv`), and the record sheet and admin log capture form and order.                                                                                                                                               |
| Asymmetric timing ("warm-up" given after tests)              | **Proposed:** every battery at the start of a session, before any test or content (T0 before pre-test, T1 at start of Session 5, T2 before post-test). Renamed "Attention and memory puzzles"; the claim that it is done "before course content" is removed from participant text. **Study team to confirm.** |
| "Latent score per component"                                 | Replaced by manifest domain summaries with reported inter-indicator correlations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ATI moderation without a comparison condition                | Reframed as association with gain unless a randomised condition exists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Stereotype-threat justification for partial disclosure       | Claim removed; the justification is reworded.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ADEXI test–retest "not widely reported"                      | Corrected to r = .68–.72, ICC .62–.72 (Holst & Thorell, 2018).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Power claim d ≈ 0.45                                         | Replaced by a simulation-based power analysis using pilot variance components.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Construct labels visible to participants                     | Participant documents have neutral titles ("Attention and memory puzzles", "About you and how you feel today"); the ADEXI is headed "How you usually are", with the attribution in small print. The administration log is a separate facilitator document.                                                    |
| Missing background covariates                                | Background questionnaire (T0) now covers language history, English proficiency, task familiarity, colour vision, vision and hearing, and handedness. An optional diagnosis item is included only with ethics approval. A facilitator administration log is given at every occasion.                           |

## Still open (needs data or decisions)

1. Calibrate the W4 switching pairs in the pilot (fruits/furniture, vegetables/clothing, jobs/vehicles are not matched in category size).
1. Cloze norming of W5 (independent sample, n ≥ 30), then replace failed items and rebalance (`ef.py --reassign-hayling`).
1. Pilot form calibration: all three forms per pilot participant in counterbalanced order; linear equating constants, retest reliability, practice gain, floor/ceiling.
1. Inter-rater agreement for W4 and W5 Part B (κ/ICC ≥ .80).
1. Cognitive interviews (5–8 learners with varied English backgrounds).
1. Confirm the proposed session timing with the study team.
1. Ethics: partial-disclosure wording, the optional diagnosis item, audio recording.
1. Check the ADEXI text against the current official PDF at chexi.se before printing.
1. Pre-register estimands, SESOIs, exclusions and analysis code.
